# ReclaimLife — Your Tree

2026-09-29 · Gobinath Manokaran · Design spec for an engineer agent. Builds on `docs/specs/2026-09-28-app-selection-positive-home-open-pause.md` (tone checklist, baseline and saved rules, the pause flow) and `docs/specs/2026-09-28-home-revamp.md` (Canopy hero, tokens, motion, accessibility). Both stay binding unless this spec changes something explicitly.

## 1. Summary

Home's Canopy hero already draws a plant that grows with "time won back since you started". This spec turns that plant into **your tree**: a companion you name, which grows overnight after each good day and for the time you win back, streaks and 2-minute swaps, from a seed to a full canopy over about five months.

**The hard rule: the tree never shrinks, wilts, dies or loses growth.** On a day over your limit, a day you raise a limit, or a day with a long pause, it **rests**. Nothing is taken away. Growth waits, and the app says so once, kindly ("Fern is taking today off. Tomorrow's a fresh start."). It's a tree, not a pet: it has no hunger, no health bar, no sad face and no red.

There is still **one plant on Home**. The six-stage `GrowthStage` plant is replaced by a ten-stage tree fed by a stored growth ledger. The tree gets bigger in the hero and gets a line of its own under it that changes with the day, and tapping either opens a **Your tree** sheet. After onboarding, a single **Meet your tree** screen explains how it grows, shows a short growth-path animation and asks for a name.

**Goals**

1. Give a reason to come back that survives a bad day. A broken streak should feel like a pause, not a reset.
2. Reward the behaviour we want: days under the limit, time won back and swaps started, without punishing pauses or limit changes (punishing them teaches people to hide choices).
3. Make the Home hero emotionally sticky: a named tree that visibly grows every week or two, with a long tail for months.
4. Keep every number honest and in agreement: streak, week strip, tree and saved figures read from the same day outcomes.

**Non-goals**

- Cosmetic species, pots, seasons or anything paid. That's a later idea (§11), out of scope.
- Paying for, buying back or "repairing" growth or streaks. Never.
- Notifications about the tree. ReclaimLife never asks for the notification permission.
- Changes to the widget, Insights, the block screen, the gate or the overlay, beyond logging swaps (§7).
- New Gradle dependencies, images, vector drawables or Lottie. The tree is drawn with `Canvas`.

**Success metrics** (on-device only, as before: there's no analytics. Read from the beta cohort's screenshots, the survey and Play Console.)

| Metric | Target | How we read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Back to growing after a rest day | ≥ 75% of testers with an over-limit day have a growing day within the next 3 days | Tree sheet "Last 7 days" strip, screenshot at day 14 and day 28 |
| D7 retention | +10% relative vs the Canopy release | Play Console |
| D30 retention | +10% relative vs the Canopy release | Play Console |
| Swaps finished | Median ≥ 3 a week per tester by week 3 | Tree sheet row "A 2-minute swap · {n} this week" (screenshot) |
| Trees named | ≥ 50% of new installs | Survey plus screenshot |
| Tone | 0 of 5 copy-test users describe any resting line as blaming | 5-user copy check before the Play release |
| Hiding | Share of testers who turn the counter off on an over-limit day doesn't rise | Survey ("Did you ever switch the counter off to avoid the tree resting?") |

## 2. Design direction

- **Grows, never shrinks.** Growth is a running total that only goes up. Days are judged once, when they close, and never re-judged.
- **Rest, not loss.** A rested day earns nothing and costs nothing. The drawing looks the same, calm and still under a moon.
- **Say it once, kindly.** At most one tree line on Home at a time (§5.2). No lists of what went wrong.
- **One tree.** The hero plant *is* the tree, for every user, with or without a baseline.
- **Reward the swap.** Habit replacement is the product. Finishing a 2-minute swap grows the tree a little.
- **Visible weekly.** Early stages come every few days. Later stages add leaves, blossoms and fruit in between, so something new shows up about every week.
- **Named, not needy.** The name makes it personal. The copy never gives the tree feelings about the user.

## 3. Growth model

All model code is pure Kotlin in `data/Growth.kt` (no Android imports), unit-tested on the JVM.

### 3.1 The growth day

- A **growth day** is keyed `yyyy-MM-dd` by **local midnight**, the same day as the reel limit, `DayHistory` and the streak. So "you went over today" and "your tree rests today" always mean the same day.
- Saved minutes for growth day `D` come from the `DailyRecord` with the same key (the ReclaimLife day that starts at `day_start_hour` on `D`). The few hours of offset are accepted (§11.4).
- Day `D` **closes for growth** once `nowMs >= nextReclaimDayStart(dayStartOfKey(D, dayStartHour))` (04:00 on `D+1` by default), after `closeOutPreviousDayIfNeeded` and `recordClosedDays` have run. Growth therefore lands **overnight**, and the tree shows it the next morning.
- A closed day is written to the ledger **once** and is never re-evaluated, whatever changes later (baseline reset, tracked apps, day start hour, limit changes).

### 3.2 What grows it

Evaluated only for a day that doesn't rest (§3.3). Points are integers.

| Source | Points | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day under your limit | **10** | Every growing day |
| Time won back | **+1 per 6 saved minutes, max +20** | `savedOnDay(record, dayStart, baselines).values.sum() / 360_000`, capped at 20. The cap keeps heavy and light users on similar timelines |
| No baseline that day | **+5 flat** | Replaces "time won back" when no baseline applies to `D` (no Usage access, or under 3 days of history). Days under the limit alone still grow the tree, just more slowly |
| 2-minute swaps | **+3 each, max 3 a day (+9)** | Swaps *finished* (the timer reached the end and the finish button was tapped) on `D`, from `swaps_by_day` |
| Streak milestone | **+15 / +30 / +45 / +60** | When the streak **through `D`** is exactly 3 / 7 / 14 / 30. Then +60 at every further multiple of 30 (60, 90, 120…). Awarded again after a streak restarts, which rewards coming back |

The streak for milestones is `currentStreak(effectiveHistory, todayMs = noon of D+1)`, the same number the streak chip showed that evening. A typical growing day is 15 points (no baseline), 17 (about 45 min saved) or 20 (with a swap).

**Swaps are not in the rest rules.** They earn nothing on a rested day, like everything else.

### 3.3 What rests it (decisions)

A closed day gets exactly one code. The first row that matches wins, and the order is also the priority when several apply.

| # | Case | Code | Decision and why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `effectiveHistory[D] == OVER`, **unless** tightening applies (below) | `O` over | Rests. The only outcome judged on reels, and the one the streak already uses |
| 2 | A limit was raised or loosened on `D` and not put back the same day | `L` limit raised | Rests. See "Limit raised" below |
| 3 | A Rest of today pause **started** on `D`, or pauses on `D` total **≥ 120 min** | `P` paused | Rests. Shorter pauses don't rest the day: their minutes are already left out of saved time, so growth pauses for exactly that period |
| 4 | No day outcome, or `DailyRecord[D]` missing, or `trackedWakingMinutes < 480` | `N` no data | Rests. The tree grows on days ReclaimLife can see. Half the waking window (8 h) must be tracked, so switching the counter off to scroll doesn't grow the tree |
| — | otherwise | `G` grew | Earns §3.2 |

**Kept exactly as today (explicitly not rests):**

- **Extras** ("just a few more" from the block screen). `dayWithinLimit` already counts `count <= limit + extras` as within, and the streak agrees. The extras ladder carries its own friction.
- **Hourly limit breaks** after a swap. Within up to `HOURLY_BREAKS_WITHIN_LIMIT` (3), as today.
- **Lowering a limit, adding a limit, tightening the mode.** Never rests.
- **Partial tracking off** (≥ 8 h tracked). Grows. Saved minutes already leave the off hours out.
- **Accessibility off right now.** Today shows a tracking-off line (§5.3). How the day closes follows the rules above.

**Limit raised.** Every change to the daily limit, hourly limit or limit mode is logged (§4.1). For day `D` (local midnight to midnight), `limitRaiseOn(changes, dayStartMs, dayEndMs, modeAtEnd)` returns a raise when:

- **Mode:** `LimitMode.valueOf(first MODE change's from).loosensTo(LimitMode.valueOf(last MODE change's to))`.
- **Daily:** `last DAILY change's to > first DAILY change's from`, and `modeAtEnd.usesDaily`.
- **Hourly:** `last HOURLY change's to > first HOURLY change's from`, and `modeAtEnd.usesHourly`.
- Priority for the returned `LimitRaise(kind, backTo)`: MODE, then DAILY, then HOURLY. `backTo` is the start-of-day value (the first change's `from`).

So **raising and then putting it back the same day clears the rest**, and the tree line says it's growing again. `modeAtEnd` is the `to` of the last MODE change at or before `dayEndMs` in the log, or the current mode when the log has none.

**Tightening never rests the tree.** If `effectiveHistory[D] == OVER`, the daily limit was **lowered** on `D`, and `DailyRecord[D]` reels total `<=` the start-of-day daily limit (the first DAILY change's `from`), the day is treated as within for growth. (Close-out judges with the *current* limit, so lowering 40 → 30 after 35 reels would otherwise count as over.)

**Pause arithmetic.** `pausedMsOn(log, dayStartMs, dayEndMs, nowMs)` is the merged overlap of `pausedIntervals(log, nowMs)` with the day. The threshold is `PAUSE_REST_THRESHOLD_MS = 120 * 60_000L`. A Rest of today entry counts only when `startMs` falls in the day and `startMs <= nowMs`; a cancelled delayed start is already removed from the log.

### 3.4 Stages

Ten stages. `thresholdPoints` is the running total needed. The "typical day" column assumes 16 points on every day plus the milestones; people with some rest days get there about 1.4× later.

| # | `TreeStage` | Label | Threshold | Typical day reached | Details within the stage (§3.5) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `SEED` | Seed | 0 | day 0 | — |
| 1 | `SPROUT` | Sprout | 15 | day 1 | — |
| 2 | `SEEDLING` | Seedling | 50 | day 3 | — |
| 3 | `YOUNG_PLANT` | Young plant | 110 | day 6 | — |
| 4 | `SAPLING` | Sapling | 240 | day 12–13 | — |
| 5 | `YOUNG_TREE` | Young tree | 420 | day 21 | 4 leaf tufts |
| 6 | `TREE` | Tree | 650 | day 31 | 4 leaf tufts |
| 7 | `BLOSSOMING` | Blossoming tree | 1100 | day 56 | 8 blossoms |
| 8 | `FRUITING` | Fruiting tree | 1700 | day 90 | 8 fruits |
| 9 | `FULL_CANOPY` | Full canopy | 2700 | day ~145 | 6 late blossoms, one per 250 points past 2700 (all shown by 4200, about month 8) |

```kotlin
enum class TreeStage(val thresholdPoints: Long, val label: String, val maxDetails: Int) { … }
fun treeStage(totalPoints: Long): TreeStage          // last stage with threshold <= total; negative → SEED
fun nextTreeStage(stage: TreeStage): TreeStage?       // null for FULL_CANOPY
fun detailsShown(stage: TreeStage, totalPoints: Long): Int
```

`detailsShown`:
- Stages 0–4: 0.
- Stages 5–8: `min(maxDetails, floor((total − threshold) / (next.threshold − threshold) × (maxDetails + 1)))`. The last detail shows before the next stage arrives.
- `FULL_CANOPY`: `min(6, (total − 2700) / 250)`.

### 3.5 Monotonic guarantees

1. **Ledger rows are append-only.** `addGrowthDays(existing, new)` ignores any key already present. No code path edits or deletes a row, except trimming (next point).
2. **Trimming carries.** The ledger keeps the newest 400 rows. When rows are dropped, their points are added to `growth_carried` in the same `edit {}`.
3. **Total** = `growth_carried + sum(row.points)`. It never goes down.
4. **Stage and details** are functions of the total that never decrease. On screen, each (stage, details) state draws at least as tall as the one before it and with at least as much canopy (§6.2, tested with `treeHeight` and `treeMass`).
5. **Nothing negative.** `evaluateGrowthDay` returns 0 or more points for every component.

### 3.6 Upgrade users, no-baseline users, baseline resets

- **Upgrade backfill** (runs once, `growth_backfilled` 0 → 1, inside `syncGrowth`):
  1. Evaluate every key in `effectiveHistory` (up to 120 days) with §3.2–3.3, using the `DailyRecord`s that exist (a missing record counts as data present for backfill only, because those days predate recording), the pause log (90 days), no limit changes (there was no log) and no swaps.
  2. **Lifetime grant:** `max(0, daysWithinLimit − count of WITHIN keys in effectiveHistory) × 10` into `growth_carried`. These are days counted before per-day history existed.
  3. **Floor from the old plant:** work out the old `GrowthStage` from the current `sinceStartedMs` and map it to a minimum new stage: SEED→SEED, SPROUT→SPROUT, SEEDLING→SEEDLING, YOUNG→YOUNG_PLANT, SAPLING→SAPLING, BLOOM→YOUNG_TREE. If the backfilled total is below that stage's threshold, add the difference to `growth_carried`. Nobody sees a smaller plant after updating.
- **No baseline** (no Usage access, or under 3 days of history): the tree grows from day points (+10), the flat +5, swaps and milestones. It shows in `Last24hHero` in place of the fixed SEED.
- **Baseline reset** (Settings, at most once in 30 days): past rows are frozen, so it can only change future "time won back" points. A baseline that appears later (Usage access granted) adds saved points from then on. Nothing is back-credited.
- **Tracked apps change, day-start change, time zone change:** rows are keyed by date and frozen, so none of these affect past growth. If a date key comes round again (a clock moved back), it's ignored because it's already in the ledger.
- **Clear storage / uninstall:** the tree starts again. That's the same as all other data.
- **Backup restore to a new phone:** the ledger comes along (§4.4).

### 3.7 Pure API (`data/Growth.kt`)

```kotlin
enum class GrowthCode(val code: Char) { GREW('G'), OVER('O'), LIMIT_RAISED('L'), PAUSED('P'), NO_DATA('N') }

data class GrowthDay(
    val dateKey: String, val code: GrowthCode,
    val dayPoints: Int = 0, val savedPoints: Int = 0, val swapPoints: Int = 0, val bonusPoints: Int = 0
) { val points: Int get() = dayPoints + savedPoints + swapPoints + bonusPoints }

data class GrowthInputs(
    val dateKey: String,
    val outcome: DayOutcome?,            // from effectiveHistory
    val record: DailyRecord?,
    val savedMs: Long?,                  // null when no baseline applies to the day
    val raise: LimitRaise?,
    val loweredDailyFrom: Int?,          // start-of-day daily limit if it was lowered that day, else null
    val restOfToday: Boolean,
    val pausedMs: Long,
    val swaps: Int,
    val streakThroughDay: Int,
    val backfill: Boolean = false
)

fun evaluateGrowthDay(inputs: GrowthInputs): GrowthDay
fun milestoneBonus(streak: Int): Int     // 3→15, 7→30, 14→45, 30→60, k·30 (k≥2)→60, else 0
fun limitRaiseOn(changes: List<LimitChange>, dayStartMs: Long, dayEndMs: Long, modeAtEnd: LimitMode): LimitRaise?
fun modeAt(changes: List<LimitChange>, atMs: Long, current: LimitMode): LimitMode
fun pausedMsOn(log: List<PauseEntry>, dayStartMs: Long, dayEndMs: Long, nowMs: Long): Long
fun quietDays(history: Map<String, DayOutcome>, records: Map<String, DailyRecord>): Set<String>
fun effectiveDayHistory(history: Map<String, DayOutcome>, records: Map<String, DailyRecord>): Map<String, DayOutcome>
fun addGrowthDays(existing: Map<String, GrowthDay>, new: Collection<GrowthDay>): Map<String, GrowthDay>
fun trimLedger(rows: Map<String, GrowthDay>, keep: Int = GROWTH_LEDGER_KEEP): Pair<Map<String, GrowthDay>, Long> // kept, carried points
fun parseGrowthLedger(raw: String?): Map<String, GrowthDay>
fun serializeGrowthLedger(rows: Map<String, GrowthDay>): String
fun legacyFloorStage(sinceStartedMs: Long): TreeStage
fun growingDaysToNext(totalPoints: Long, rows: Map<String, GrowthDay>): Int?   // null at FULL_CANOPY
fun stageReachedOn(rows: Map<String, GrowthDay>, carried: Long, stage: TreeStage): String? // key of the row that crossed it

const val GROWTH_LEDGER_KEEP = 400
const val PAUSE_REST_THRESHOLD_MS = 120 * 60_000L
const val MIN_TRACKED_MINUTES_TO_GROW = 480
const val GROWTH_CLOSE_WINDOW_DAYS = 14
```

- **`quietDays`** returns dates missing from `DayHistory` whose `DailyRecord` has `trackedWakingMinutes >= 480` and 0 reels in total. The counter was running and nothing was watched, so it's a WITHIN day. `effectiveDayHistory = history + quietDays.associateWith { WITHIN }` (existing entries win).
- **`growingDaysToNext`**: the pace is the average points of the last 14 `G` rows (with fewer than 3 such rows, 15). The result is `ceil(remaining / pace)`, at least 1.
- **`stageReachedOn`** walks the rows in date order from `carried`, and returns the key where the running total first reaches the stage's threshold, or null if `carried` alone reaches it.

### 4.2 Day outcome gaps (prerequisite fix)

Today `rollDateIfNeeded` resets the count at midnight **without** recording the outgoing day's outcome. The outcome is recorded only by `closeOutPreviousDayIfNeeded` (app open, service connect). So whenever the service stays connected past midnight and a reel is counted before the app opens, that day is missing from `day_history`. The streak skips it, and without this fix the tree would rest on it.

- Every public `ReelUsageRepository` method that calls `rollDateIfNeeded` (`incrementAndGet`, `grantExtraAndGet`, `clearHourlyWindow`, `recordExtraAttemptAndGet`) gains `dailyLimit: Int, mode: LimitMode` parameters. `rollDateIfNeeded(prefs, today, dailyLimit, mode)` records the stored date's outcome (the same code as `closeOutPreviousDayIfNeeded`, pulled into `private fun recordOutcome(prefs, storedDate, limit, mode)`) before it resets. Callers (the service and `BlockActivity`) already read both settings.
- **Quiet days** (no reels, app never opened) are covered by `effectiveDayHistory` (§3.7). Home's `WeekSection` and `StreakChip` use `effectiveDayHistory` too, so the strip, streak and tree agree. `Progress` carries `quietDays: Set<String>`, and `HomeScreen` merges them into `dayHistory` before passing it on.

### 4.3 Where it's computed

- **New `service/GrowthEngine.kt`**:
  - `suspend fun syncGrowth(app, nowMs)` is called at the end of `syncProgressData`, after `ensureBaselines`, under the same `syncLock`. First it calls `closeOutPreviousDayIfNeeded(currentLimit, currentMode)`. Then it backfills if needed, and evaluates every closeable key from `max(firstEligibleKey, today − 14)` up to the last closeable key that's missing from the ledger. `firstEligibleKey` is the earlier of the first `day_history` key and the date of `recording_since_ms`. After that it trims, carries and writes in one `edit {}`.
  - `suspend fun buildTreeState(app, nowMs): TreeState` is called from `computeProgress`.
- **`Progress`** gains `val tree: TreeState` and `val quietDays: Set<String>`. The widget and pause calm screen ignore them. Update any test that constructs `Progress`.
- **`TreeState`** (pure data class in `data/Growth.kt`):

## 5. States and call-outs

### 5.1 Today's state

`todayRest(...)` is pure and returns `RestReason?`. The first match wins:

| # | `RestReason` | When (today = local midnight day) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `OVER_LIMIT` | `!dayWithinLimit(mode, todayCount, dailyLimit, extras, hourlyBreaks)`, and tightening (§3.3) doesn't apply. Needs a new `todayHourlyBreaks` flow on `ReelUsageRepository` |
| 2 | `LIMIT_RAISED` | `limitRaiseOn(changes, todayStart, now, mode) != null` |
| 3 | `PAUSED_DAY` | A Rest of today pause started today (running or ended), or `pausedMsOn(today) >= 120 min` |
| 4 | `PAUSED_NOW` | A 15 min / 1 hour pause is running now (`pausedFromMs <= now < pausedUntilMs`) |
| 5 | `TRACKING_OFF` | `!isReelBlockerServiceRunning(app)` |

`PAUSED_NOW` and `TRACKING_OFF` are periods. When the pause ends, or counting comes back, the tree goes back to growing unless a day rule applies. A pending Rest of today (in its 60 s delayed start) is not a rest yet.

### 5.2 Call-out priority (one at a time)

`treeCallout(state, name, todayKey, localHour): TreeCallout` is pure. `data class TreeCallout(val kind: CalloutKind, val text: String, val glyph: CalloutGlyph, val action: CalloutAction?)`. The first match wins:

| # | `CalloutKind` | Condition | Glyph |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `RESTING` | `todayRest != null` (text by reason) | Moon |
| 2 | `NEW_STAGE` | `stageReachedOn(current stage)` is the most recent closed ledger key | Star |
| 3 | `MILESTONE` | The most recent closed row has `bonusPoints > 0` | Star |
| 4 | `FIRST_DAY` | No `G` rows ever and `growth_carried == 0` | Leaf |
| 5 | `BACK_AFTER_REST` | The most recent closed row is rested (`O`/`L`/`P`/`N`) | Leaf |
| 6 | `GREW_OVERNIGHT` | The most recent closed row is `G` and `localHour < 12` | Leaf |
| 7 | `GROWING` | Otherwise | Leaf |

"Most recent closed row" means the ledger row for yesterday's key. If that key has no row (the app was closed for days), rules 2, 3, 5 and 6 are skipped. **Resting outranks a celebration**, because the current state is what the person needs to know. The celebration was visible for the rest of the day, and the stage stays in the sheet.

`CalloutAction` exists only for `RESTING` + `LIMIT_RAISED`: `SetBack(kind, backTo)`. It shows in the sheet, not on Home (Home keeps a single tap target per line).

### 5.3 Messages

- `{Name}` = `tree_name` or **"Your tree"**, and `{name}` = `tree_name` or **"your tree"** (mid-sentence).
- Where a state has several lines, pick `pool[epochDay(todayKey) % pool.size]`, where `epochDay` is days since 1970-01-01 from the date key. The line stays the same all day, never flickers, and varies across days. `epochDay` is pure (parse with `SimpleDateFormat("yyyy-MM-dd", Locale.US)` in UTC).

| GREW_OVERNIGHT | "{Name} grew overnight. Yesterday counted." · "New growth this morning, from yesterday." · "Yesterday helped {name} grow. Here's to today." |
| BACK_AFTER_REST | "Fresh day. {Name} is growing again." · "{Name} is awake and growing again." · "New day, new growth for {name}." |

| NEW_STAGE | "{Name} {stage.nowPhrase}!" (see §8, one per stage) |
| MILESTONE | "{n} days under your limit. {Name} got an extra boost." |

| RESTING · LIMIT_RAISED (mode) | "{Name} is resting today while the {dropped} limit is off. Switch it back on and it grows again." ({dropped} = "daily" / "hourly") |

| RESTING · PAUSED_NOW | "{Name} rests while you're paused." · "{Name} is resting during your pause." |
| RESTING · TRACKING_OFF | "{Name} grows again once counting is back on." |

**Tone checklist for every tree line:** it frames what's next, never what went wrong. It gives the tree no feelings about the user (no "misses you", "sad", "hungry", "thirsty"). It never says the tree loses, shrinks, wilts, dies or is hurt. No red, no "warning", no numbers of days missed, and at most one constructive offer. A unit test (Task 4) fails if any pool line matches, case-insensitively and at a word start (`Regex("\\b(die|dead|…)", IGNORE_CASE)`, so "close" doesn't trip "lose"), any of: `die`, `dead`, `dying`, `wilt`, `shrink`, `lost`, `lose`, `fail`, `kill`, `sad`, `hungry`, `thirsty`, `miss`, `guilt`, `shame`, `warning`, `broke`, `ruin`. The only allowed exception is the sheet's reassurance line "never shrinks", which isn't in a pool.

### 6.3 Motion

Add to `object Motion`: `treeGrowMs = 900`, `treeDetailMs = 400`, `treeCelebrateMs = 1200`, `treeSwayMs = 5000`, `treeSwayDegrees = 1.2f`, `introStripStaggerMs = 250`, `seedDropMs = 500`.

- **Grow-in (first composition in a process, as today):** one `Animatable` p from 0 → 1 over `treeGrowMs`, `FastOutSlowInEasing`.
  - Stages 0–4 use the existing plant mapping.
  - Stages 5–9: trunk height × `min(1, p/0.4)`. Branch i length × `clamp((p − 0.3 − i·0.05)/0.2)`. Cluster i scales about its centre by `clamp((p − 0.35 − i·0.05)/0.3)`. Detail i scales by `clamp((p − 0.7 − i·0.03)/0.2)`.
- **Stage-up** (the stage differs from `lastAnimatedTree`): **never from empty**. Draw the previous stage at full size underneath, grow the new stage over it with the mapping above, and fade the previous stage out over the last 30% of p. The new stage always has more mass (§6.2), so it never dips.
- **New details, same stage:** only the details with index ≥ the last animated count scale in, over `treeDetailMs` each, 80ms apart.
- **Celebration** (`celebrate = true`, meaning the call-out is NEW_STAGE or MILESTONE, once per process per ledger key; keep `private var celebratedKey: String?`): after the grow finishes, 8 small leaves (the leaf path at L = 5u) rise from the top cluster's centre, spread over −60°…+60°. They rise 28u and fade from 1 → 0 over `treeCelebrateMs`, alternating `plantLeafBright` / `plantBloom`. The call-out glyph disc scales 1 → 1.15 → 1 over 300ms, once.
- **Idle sway** (growing, not resting, `animate = true`, no reduced motion): the whole drawing rotates about B between −1.2° and +1.2°, `infiniteRepeatable(tween(treeSwayMs, easing = FastOutSlowInEasing), RepeatMode.Reverse)`. It runs only while composed. Resting has no sway, which reads as still and calm.
- **Process memory:** `private var lastAnimatedTree: Pair<TreeStage, Int>? = null` in `GrowthTree.kt` replaces `lastAnimatedStage`. A cold start regrows it, which is intended, as before.
- **Reduced motion** (`rememberReducedMotion()`): the final state, no sway, no particles, no seed drop. The moon appears without a fade.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

**Growth model**

- **Given** a day under the limit with 45 min saved and one swap, **when** the day closes at 04:00, **then** the ledger gains `G:10:7:3:0` for that date, and the total rises by 20.
- **Given** a day over the limit, **then** its row is `O`, the total is unchanged, and no stored value decreases.
- **Given** the daily limit raised from 30 to 40 at 14:00 and set back to 30 at 18:00, **then** the day grows if otherwise within, and between 14:00 and 18:00 the hero line reads "…Back to 30 and it grows again."
- **Given** two 1-hour pauses on one day, **then** the day rests (`P`). **Given** one 15 min pause, **then** the day still grows and the hero line reads a PAUSED_NOW line only while it runs.
- **Given** a Rest of today pause, **then** that local day is `P`, and the hero line reads a PAUSED_DAY line from the moment it starts.
- **Given** Accessibility off for 10 waking hours, **then** the day is `N`.
- **Given** the limit lowered from 40 to 30 after 35 reels, **then** the tree still grows that day.
- **Given** a user with no Usage access keeping every day under the limit, **then** the tree reaches SPROUT after the first closed day (15 points).
- **Given** a streak reaching 7 on a growing day, **then** that row's bonus is 30, and the next morning's line is "7 days under your limit. {Name} got an extra boost." (unless the tree is resting today or reached a new stage).
- **Given** a baseline reset, **then** no ledger row changes.
- **Given** an upgrade with an old plant at BLOOM, **then** the tree is at least YOUNG_TREE on first launch.
- **Given** 401 ledger rows, **then** 400 are kept, and `growth_carried` grows by the oldest row's points. The total is unchanged.

**Home and hero**

- **Given** any loaded Home, **then** there is exactly one plant drawing on the screen: the tree, in the hero.
- **Given** a growing day, **then** the hero shows the tree at 136×180dp swaying gently, with the row "{Name} · {Stage}" and a GROWING, GREW_OVERNIGHT or BACK_AFTER_REST line. **Given** a resting day, **then** the same drawing shows without sway, with a moon and a dimmer sun, and a RESTING line. It is never smaller, paler or drooping.
- **Given** a new stage reached overnight, **when** Home opens, **then** the tree grows from the previous stage (never from empty), leaves rise from the canopy once, and the line reads "{Name} is a sapling now!" (etc.).
- **Given** a rested day and a new stage on the same morning, **then** only the resting line shows.
- **Given** a tap on the tree or the call-out row, **then** the Your tree sheet opens. A tap elsewhere on the saved hero opens "Time won back".

**Sheet and intro**

- **Given** the sheet, **then** it shows the name, stage n of 10, the progress to the next stage with "About {n} growing days", the stage note, 7 days with leaf/moon marks and "Grew {n} of the last 7 days", what helps it grow (with this week's swaps) and the never-shrinks line.

## 11. Open questions and risks

1. **Cosmetic species and seasons (monetisation).** Out of scope. If they come later, they can be paid for **appearance only** (another species, blossom colour, a pot). Growth, stages, streaks and repairs are never sold. Decided in principle; the design is for later.
2. **Widget line** ("Fern is growing today"). Left out of this release so the widget stays a budget glance. Revisit after beta if testers ask for it.
3. **Point tuning.** The stage timings assume about 16 points a day. Rows freeze their points, so retuning later affects only future days and can never shrink anyone's tree. Check in the 2-week beta whether most testers reach SEEDLING by day 4. If not, lower the thresholds; never raise existing ones above what a user has.
4. **Midnight vs 04:00.** Growth days follow the limit's midnight day, while saved minutes follow the 04:00 ReclaimLife day. A pause at 00:30 counts toward the new calendar day. Accepted, because it keeps "today" identical for the limit, the streak and the tree.
5. **Streak and tree can differ on edge days.** A day within the limit but rested for a raise or a long pause still counts for the streak and still earns streak milestones on later growing days. That's intended: the streak is about reels, and the tree is about the whole day.
6. **Extras don't rest the tree.** Decided to match the streak and `dayWithinLimit`. If beta shows the extras ladder being used to "save" the tree, revisit with the lead designer. Don't change it silently.
7. **Gaming by switching the counter off.** Mitigated by the 8-hour tracked minimum (`N`) and by saved minutes that leave off-time out. The survey question in §1 watches for it.
8. **The intro adds a screen to first run** (tension with UX #17). It's after onboarding, has one action, the name is optional, and back works. If the completion rate from Ready to Home drops by more than 5% in beta, show the intro on the second app open instead.
9. **Hero number wrapping.** The bigger tree narrows the text column to about 187dp, so "10 h 45 min" wraps to two lines at 100% font. Accepted. If beta screenshots look cramped, reduce HERO to 128×170dp before touching the type scale.
10. **`isReelBlockerServiceRunning` cost.** It's called once per `computeProgress` (every minute on Home). It's already used there, so no new cost.
